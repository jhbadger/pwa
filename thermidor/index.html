Thermidor

The French Republican Calendar was used in France from 1793 to 1805. Each of the twelve 30-day months is divided into three 10-day weeks (décades), with 5 or 6 extra "Sansculottides" days at year's end. Rather than saints' days, each day of the year is named for a plant, animal, or tool of rural life. The decimal clock divides the day into 10 hours of 100 decimal minutes of 100 decimal seconds.
